---
name: rewrite
description: Rewrite text in a different tone or length, like friendlier, shorter, more formal or simpler.
---
1. If the user didn't say how, ask once: friendlier, shorter, more formal, or simpler?
2. Keep the meaning, names, numbers and dates exactly as they are.
3. Give only the rewritten text, ready to copy. After it, one line on what changed.
4. For long text, keep the same headings and order.
