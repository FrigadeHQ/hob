---
name: reply
description: Draft a reply to an email or message the user pastes or shares.
---
1. Work out what the message asks for and what the user needs to say. If the user's answer isn't clear (yes or no, a date, a price), ask once.
2. Match the sender's tone: casual for casual, polite and clear for work.
3. Keep it short: answer the questions, say any next step, and close kindly.
4. Give only the reply, ready to copy, with a subject line if it's an email.
