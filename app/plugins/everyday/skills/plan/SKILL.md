---
name: plan
description: Break a goal or big task into clear steps with a realistic timeline.
---
1. If you don't know the goal, the deadline and how much time they have, ask for them in one message.
2. Split it into 4 to 10 steps, each small enough to start today. Put them in order.
3. For each step: what to do, about how long it takes, and when to do it.
4. Point out the hardest step and how to make it easier. Keep it encouraging and realistic.
