---
name: meeting-notes
description: Turn rough meeting notes or a transcript into a clean summary with decisions and action items.
---
1. Read everything first.
2. Write it up as:
   - Summary: 2 to 4 sentences.
   - Decisions: what was agreed.
   - Action items: a table with task, owner and due date (write "not set" when it's missing).
   - Open questions: anything left unanswered.
3. Use names exactly as they appear. Don't invent owners or dates.
4. If the user wants a file, save it as a Word document in the chat's folder.
