---
name: summarize
description: Summarize a document, article, chat or notes into the key points and anything to act on.
---
1. Read all of it first (use read_file or read_pdf for files).
2. Write 3 to 7 short bullet points with the main ideas, most important first.
3. Add "What to do" with any actions, deadlines or decisions. Leave it out if there are none.
4. Keep the user's own words for names, numbers and dates. Don't add facts that aren't there.
