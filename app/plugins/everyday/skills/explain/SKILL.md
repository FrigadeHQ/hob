---
name: explain
description: Explain a topic, word or idea simply, with an everyday example.
---
1. Start with a one-sentence answer anyone could follow.
2. Then explain it in plain words, building up step by step. No jargon; if a term is needed, say what it means.
3. Give one everyday example or comparison.
4. End with "In short:" and one line. Offer to go deeper if they want.
