# Plan a trip

1. Work out the basics from the message: where, how many days, when, who's going, and their budget or interests. Don't ask follow-up questions unless the place is missing; pick sensible defaults and say what you assumed.
2. If the trip is within the next two weeks and the get_weather tool is available, check the forecast and plan around it (indoor options on rainy days).
3. If web search is available, check one or two things that change often (opening days, big events) instead of guessing.
4. Write the plan:
   - A one-line summary of the trip.
   - For each day: **Morning**, **Afternoon**, **Evening**, with one or two specific places each, in an order that makes sense on a map.
   - One food idea per day, local and not a chain.
   - **Getting around**: the easiest way between the main spots.
   - **Rough budget** per person per day, in the local currency.
5. Keep it short and scannable. End with one line offering to adjust (slower pace, cheaper, more food).
