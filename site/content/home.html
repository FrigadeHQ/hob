A few things to know.

What kind of computer do I need?

Hob runs on Apple Silicon Macs, Windows PCs, and Linux, with at least 8 GB of memory (RAM). More memory lets Hob run a smarter model: 16 GB is good for everyday tasks, and 32 GB or more gets you the best ones. Hob picks the best model your computer can run for you.

Is Hob as capable as Claude or ChatGPT?

On a powerful computer, close to it. With 32 GB of memory or more, Hob runs the same kind of open models the big AI labs build, and for everyday work like writing, summaries, spreadsheets, and research it feels much like ChatGPT or Claude. On smaller computers, Hob uses a lighter model that’s great for quick tasks. Either way, you can chat, create documents, analyze data, run code, and work with connected tools, all without sending your files anywhere.

What stays on my computer?

Your model runs locally, and your chats and files are stored on your computer. Hob has no telemetry. Web tools and connectors send the information needed for their actions to external services; those services have their own privacy policies. You choose which connectors to add.

When do I need an internet connection?

You need a connection to download Hob and its model, install updates, and use web tools or connected services. Some tasks may also need to download software packages. Chat and work with files already on your computer can run offline.

Are there any extra costs?

Hob and local model use have no subscription or per-message charge. Optional external services you choose to connect may have their own fees.
